Ebi Ten Udon Recipe

Ebi ten Udon with chicken broth, seaweed, and chunks of chicken with a side of tempura.

Chicken, udon noodles, mushrooms, bok choy, and mung bean sprouts come together in this savory soup.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ (32 fluid ounce) containers chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger root
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 large cooked skinless, boneless chicken breast, chopped
  • 1 head bok choy, chopped
  • ¼ cup dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 2 (7 ounce) packages dried udon noodles
  • ½ cup mung bean sprouts
  • 1 green onion, sliced diagonally
  • 2 tablespoons dried minced onion
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
